Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who we are

Clip City is a private tool operated by its owner to publish short gaming clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

2. What we access

When the owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account, the tool receives only what the owner authorizes: basic profile information (such as the account name) and permission to upload and publish videos. We do not access private messages, contacts, or any data about other users.

3. What we store

We store authorization tokens, clip files and clip metadata (titles, captions, view counts) on the owner's private server. This information is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Third-party services

The tool uses third-party services to operate, including TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, Telegram, and AI providers that process video transcripts and frames to find highlights. These services receive only the data needed to perform their function.

5. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete stored clips and tokens at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the settings of TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) and Google (Security, Third-party access).
